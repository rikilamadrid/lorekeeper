Status-reading standups fail because the information is already written down
somewhere. The meeting exists to make people feel synchronised, not to
synchronise them.

Mentioned this to [[Jane Okafor]] in passing; she's seen it work async at a
previous company. Also came up while I was thinking about [[Pathfinder]] and
whether written handoffs beat verbal ones.
